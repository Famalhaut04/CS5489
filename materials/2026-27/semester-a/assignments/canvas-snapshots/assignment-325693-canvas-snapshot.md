## 作业说明快照

This assignment aims to enable the group registration in People Page under the tag Groups.


Group Registration Instructions: We have created 150 groups for you, and specifically, the students who would like to form a group may select the same empty group to join. Please make sure that the group is tagged with "Project". Meanwhile, the groups can be formed across different class sessions, i.e., daytime and evening students are permitted and welcome to work together.


Please make sure to register on the pre-created group. Kindly note that any group generated by yourself CANNOT be linked to any assignment, and submissions made to such a group will not be received or graded.


Meanwhile, if your group continues to experience technical issues with the registration process, you may submit a single PDF document containing the following details:



Your group's name


All the group members' full names, student IDs, and email addresses



such that we will manually complete the registration for you. Please note that only one submission is required per group, and this is unnecessary if you have already registered successfully.
