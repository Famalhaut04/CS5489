## 作业说明快照

You should submit both code file and video for your final course project. For your video presentation, we will use Canvas/Panopto and your presentation can also be viewed by others. Similarly, you can also leave comments on your fellow student's presentations.



Code Submission instructions


Please submit your code in a single, well-organized zipfile with the name ProjectGroup_[YourGroupNumber]_Code.zip.


The code should include all necessary files to run your project.


A README.txt or README.md file with clear instructions.


Where to download the data: Provide the exact URL or the name of the dataset from a public repository


How to preprocess it: List any commands or scripts needed to prepare the data


Where to place it: Specify the exact folder structure





Important Notes on Data:


Do NOT include large datasets in your zip file, especially if they are publicly available. 








Video Submission instructions


First, use Panopto to record your video (or you can record first, and upload to Panopto). You can also use Panopto to edit/trim the video.  


You should save your video to the folder "2025/26 Semester A - CS5489 Machine Learning:Algorit &Apns [assignments]".


Please set the title of your video as the following "Group X: Y", where X is your group number, and Y is your project topic.


Second, in the text entry box of this assignment submission, select the following from the toolbox: Apps (the electric plug icon -> Panopto Recordings.  Then choose the video to embed.


Detailed instructions can be found here(CityU) and here (Canvas).


Presentation video are limited to 5 minutes.  Remember to mention your Project Group number, and put your Project Group number in the video name.





After the due date, each student should watch at least 3 videos and comment on them or ask questions.


The due date for uploading your video is Dec. 1 @ 11:59pm.
